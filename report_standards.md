# Report Standards for Leadership Status Reports

1. **Lead with the headline.** The first 2-3 sentences must tell a reader the overall state and the single most important ask.
2. **Plain language.** No jargon, no filler. Write for a busy executive who has 3 minutes.
3. **RAG status with a reason.** Give Red, Amber, or Green, plus one or two sentences on why. Never give a color without a justification.
4. **Numbers must match the data export.** If a number in the notes differs from the metrics export, use the export and flag the discrepancy.
5. **Every risk has an owner and a date.** If either is missing in the sources, say so explicitly: "Owner not assigned."
6. **Decisions are asks, not mentions.** State what is needed, from whom, and by when.
7. **Flag changes.** Anything that changed since last month (dates, scope, budget) must be called out plainly, not buried.
8. **Do not speculate.** Distinguish what the sources say from your own inference, and label inferences as such.
